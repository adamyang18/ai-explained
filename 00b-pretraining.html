Pretraining:
an AI's “school years”

The first step in making an AI assistant, and the most expensive one.

IN ONE LINE

Take a blank brain and have it read a massive amount of human writing

The “pre” means laying the foundation first. After this come fine-tuning and human feedback, and only then does it become the chat assistant you use.

STEP 1 · STOCKING UP

Collect text, then filter out the junk

You are what you eat. Data quality often matters more than data quantity.

STEP 2 · PRACTICE QUESTIONS

One question, over and over: guess the next word

The answer key is just the original text, so nobody has to write the questions or grade them. That's why it can use such a massive amount of text.

STEP 3 · REPEAT UNTIL THE END OF TIME

One word, one question. Trillions of them.

This is the most expensive, most power-hungry part of building a model.

BONUS SKILLS

To guess well, it was forced to learn a lot

Nobody taught it these on purpose. They're side effects of trying to guess the next word right.

ON GRADUATION DAY

Knows a ton, but can't hold a conversation

Pretraining only teaches it to keep writing. To “understand the question and answer it properly,” it needs fine-tuning afterwards.

LIMITS

The day it finishes reading, time stops

So when you ask about recent news, prices or rules, have it search, or check for yourself.

The short version

Pretraining = having a model read a massive amount of text and guess the next word over and over, until you get a base model that “knows a little about everything, but can't be an assistant yet.”
